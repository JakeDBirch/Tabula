Loud Light

Privacy policy · last updated 22 September 2026

Loud Light is a grid sequencer. It is made by one person, it has no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking of any kind, and it does not sell or share anything about you with anybody.

This policy covers the iOS app and the web app at jakedbirch.github.io/Tabula.

What stays on your device

By default, everything. Your projects — patterns, songs, sounds, kits and any samples you record — are stored on the device in the app's own storage. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you sign in and choose to save to the cloud. The app works completely offline.

What is sent, and only if you sign in

Signing in is optional. The app is fully usable without an account, and without one it makes no network requests at all. If you do sign in:

  • Your email address. Used to send you a one-time sign-in code and to own your projects. There is no password. We do not email you anything else — no newsletters, no marketing.
  • Projects you choose to save to the cloud. A project is your musical work: patterns, arrangement, sound settings, and any samples you recorded with the microphone, all in one file. Only projects you explicitly save with the CLOUD tab are uploaded. Saving to DEVICE sends nothing.

That data is held in a Supabase database acting as the app's backing store, readable only by your own account. It is not used for anything except giving your projects back to you.

The microphone

The drum sampler can record a short sample from the microphone when you tap REC on a drum voice. iOS asks your permission the first time, and you can refuse or revoke it in Settings; the rest of the app works either way. A recording is stored in your project like any other sound, and leaves your device only if you save that project to the cloud.

What is never collected

  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage statistics.
  • No advertising, no advertising identifier, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No location, no contacts, no photos, no health data, no camera.
  • No third-party SDKs. The app talks to exactly one host, and only when you are signed in.

Keeping it, and deleting it

Cloud projects are kept until you delete them. You can delete any one of them from the CLOUD list inside the app.

You can also delete your whole account from inside the app — PROJECT ▸ CLOUD ▸ DELETE ACCOUNT. That removes the account and every project stored under it, immediately and permanently. It cannot be undone, and it does not touch anything saved on your device.

Children

Loud Light is not directed at children and collects nothing that identifies a person beyond the email address used to sign in.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. The app is open source, so the history of this page is public at github.com/JakeDBirch/Tabula.
